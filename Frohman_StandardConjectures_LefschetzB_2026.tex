\begin{abstract}
We record a clean, zero-\texttt{sorry} Lean 4 module (\texttt{StandardConjectures.lean}) formalizing the classical implication between the rational Hodge conjecture on the self-product $X \times X$ of a smooth projective variety and Grothendieck's Lefschetz Standard Conjecture $B(X)$. Specifically, let $\Lambda$ denote the inverse Lefschetz operator $L^{-1}$ on primitive cohomology viewed as a correspondence class on $X \times X$. If $\Lambda$ is a rational Hodge class and the Hodge conjecture holds on $X \times X$ in the degree of $\Lambda$, then $\Lambda$ is algebraic (\texttt{LefschetzB $\Lambda$}). We explicitly separate this implication from the trivial algebraicity of the forward Lefschetz hyperplane cup operator $L = \smile h$ (\texttt{L\_is\_algebraic}) and document that neither Grothendieck's Conjecture B nor the Clay Hodge Conjecture is unconditionally discharged here.
\end{abstract}

\section{Formalized Theorem Statement}
\begin{theorem}[\texttt{hodge\_on\_product\_implies\_lefschetz\_B}]
Let $\Lambda : \mathrm{Class}$ represent the K\"unneth/correspondence class of the inverse Lefschetz operator on $X \times X$. Under the hypotheses $\mathrm{h\Lambda} : \mathrm{IsHodge}(\Lambda)$ and $\mathrm{hHC} : \forall \gamma,\, \mathrm{IsHodge}(\gamma) \to \mathrm{IsAlgebraic}(\gamma)$ on $X \times X$:
\[
\mathrm{IsHodge}(\Lambda) \;\wedge\; \mathrm{HodgeOnProduct} \implies \mathrm{LefschetzB}(\Lambda).
\]
\end{theorem}
